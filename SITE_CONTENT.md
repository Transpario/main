# 1. Content Purpose

This document contains the approved baseline copy and content direction for the Transpario website.

The content should communicate Transpario as a neutral, student-focused internship transparency platform.

The website should not sound aggressive, accusatory, sensational, or promotional.

The core objective is simple:

> Help students understand what they are choosing before they apply.

---

# 2. Brand Voice

Transpario's voice should be:

- Clear
- Direct
- Neutral
- Professional
- Student-friendly
- Informative
- Independent
- Evidence-conscious

## Avoid

Do not use language such as:

- "Expose fake internships"
- "Scam companies"
- "Fraud internships"
- "Bad companies"
- "We catch scammers"
- "Don't get scammed"
- "We rate companies"
- "We blacklist companies"

unless explicitly approved under a future policy.

The platform should communicate information rather than make inflammatory accusations.

# 48. Methodology / Guidelines Page

## Heading

> OUR GUIDELINES

## Intro

> Transpario is built around a simple principle: present useful information without unnecessary assumptions.

## Guideline 01

### FACTS OVER ACCUSATIONS

> Public information should focus on what can be supported rather than using inflammatory labels.

## Guideline 02

### NO ASSUMPTIONS

> Missing information is not automatically treated as a negative result.

## Guideline 03

### STUDENT EXPERIENCES STAY PERSONAL

> A student's experience is presented as that student's experience. It is not automatically treated as a universal fact.

## Guideline 04

### INFORMATION CAN CHANGE

> Internship programs can change their policies, pricing, selection processes, and benefits. This is why Transpario displays a Last Reviewed date.

## Guideline 05

### EVIDENCE MATTERS

> Supporting information can help the Transpario team understand and review factual claims.

## Guideline 06

### PRIVACY MATTERS

> Personal information and sensitive evidence should not be exposed publicly by default.

## Guideline 07

### PUBLICATION REQUIRES REVIEW

> Student submissions do not automatically become public listings.

# 49. Methodology — Classification Explanation

## Heading

> WHAT DO OUR CLASSIFICATIONS MEAN?

### No Fee

> No payment from the student is currently identified as required for the internship program.

### Payment Required

> Available information indicates that payment from the student is required.

### Optional Payment

> A payment option exists, but the available information does not indicate that it is mandatory for participation in the internship itself.

### Unclear

> Transpario does not currently have enough information to confidently classify the payment requirement.

### Not Disclosed

> Transpario does not currently have sufficient information to make a stronger classification.

---

# 50. Methodology — Important Distinction

## Heading

> NOT DISCLOSED DOES NOT MEAN NO.

## Body

> When Transpario does not have enough reliable information about a feature, we use "Not Disclosed" rather than assuming that the feature does not exist.

This applies especially to:

- Stipends
- Certificates
- Mentorship
- Selection processes
- Work details

# 51. FAQ

## What is Transpario?

> Transpario is an internship transparency platform that helps students understand internship opportunities through structured information and real student experiences.

## Does Transpario rate companies?

> No. The MVP does not use company ratings, star scores, or numerical rankings.

## Does Transpario call internships scams?

> Transpario is designed to present neutral, factual information rather than automatically labeling programs as scams or frauds.

## What does "Payment Required" mean?

> It means the available information indicates that payment from the student is required for the relevant internship program or participation.

## What does "Not Disclosed" mean?

> It means Transpario does not currently have enough information to make a stronger classification. It does not automatically mean the feature does not exist.

## Are student reviews verified?

> Submissions are reviewed by the Transpario team. Where relevant, supporting evidence may be considered. A published student experience still represents an individual's experience.

## Can I submit an internship experience?

> Yes. Use the "Review an Internship" form to submit your experience.

## Will my submission be published automatically?

> No. Submissions are reviewed before any information is published.

## Can I submit anonymously?

> Yes, where the review workflow allows anonymous publication. Contact information may still be requested privately for clarification.

## Does Transpario charge companies to be listed?

> The MVP is designed around independent information and does not use paid placement as a basis for public classification.

## Can internship information change?

> Yes. Internship programs can change over time. Transpario therefore displays a Last Reviewed date.

# 61. Content Rules for Developers

Developers must not:

- Invent company information
- Invent internship details
- Invent stipend amounts
- Invent payment amounts
- Invent reviews
- Invent verification claims
- Turn missing data into negative classifications
- Generate fake student experiences
- Add unsupported testimonials

If information is missing, display the appropriate neutral state.

---

# 62. Content Rules for AI

AI-generated content must not be presented as a real student experience or verified fact.

AI may assist with:

- Formatting
- Summarization
- Drafting neutral descriptions

But human review is required before public publication of factual classifications or student experiences.

# 65. Legal / Neutrality Disclaimer

Use a concise disclaimer where appropriate:

> Transpario presents information based on available sources and reviewed student experiences. Information may change over time. Student experiences represent individual perspectives and should not be treated as universal conclusions.

This should not dominate the UI.

---

# 66. Final Content Principle

Every important piece of copy should pass three questions:

### 1. Is it clear?

A student should understand it quickly.

### 2. Is it neutral?

The wording should not make unsupported accusations or assumptions.

### 3. Is it useful?

The content should help a student understand an internship before applying.

If a piece of copy fails one of these three tests, rewrite it.

---

# 67. Final Brand Message

Transpario is not here to decide for students.

It is here to make information easier to understand.

The final communication should always lead back to:

> **KNOW BEFORE YOU APPLY.**

And the central philosophy:

> **We don't tell students what to choose. We help them understand what they're choosing.**
