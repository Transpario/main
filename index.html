Why Transpario?

# Accuracy First

We document factual parameters like cost, training fees, and stipend. Personal opinions and assumptions are kept out of our database.

# Strict Neutrality

Transpario is not a blacklist. We classify opportunities objectively. If an internship charges fees upfront, we label it and let students decide.

# Absolute Privacy

We redact and mask all student identities, contact details, Aadhaar/Voter IDs, and UPI handles before verification logs are posted.

Contributor Roadmap

01

Apply

Fill out our verification form. Government ID is required (Aadhaar or Voter ID preferred) to verify Indian nationality.

02

Verify

Review the Transpario Guide, Code of Conduct, and complete a brief 10-minute online screening call with a moderator.

03

Mentorship

Start as a Junior Investigator. Draft audits and verify cases with double-review oversight from your senior mentor.

04

Investigate

Attain full investigator status. Manage review cases independently, assign Transparency Scores, and maintain integrity.
